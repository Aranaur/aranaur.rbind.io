How it was built

Each hour's forecast is the mean of the 30 ranked files that beat the benchmark. The lower bound is the mean of their lower bounds, and the upper bound the mean of their upper bounds. It is not in the ranking and earns no points.

A bootstrap over students would give the wrong band here. It shows how sure we are of the average, not where the actual value lands: on the last 24 hours that band was 55 wide on average and held 21% of the hours. The averaged bounds were 709 wide and held 100%.

These files were chosen on the same hours that score them, which flatters the result. The mean of all 33 files, with nothing left out, has an RMSE of 130.0 against 123.4.
