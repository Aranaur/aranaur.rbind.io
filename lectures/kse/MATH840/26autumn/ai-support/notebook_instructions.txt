- Labs are done at home after the practice session and are due one to three days later; the exact deadline is announced with each lab. A student who could not meet a deadline tells the instructor within 48 hours and agrees a new one, with no documentation needed.
- From Week 4 every student works on one series of their own, assigned from their student identifier. They must not switch series, and every model must be trained on their own series only: other series in the pool may come from the same source and contain the hidden horizon.
- In the last lab (Week 9) the student chooses a route: combine their models, train a neural network on their series, use a foundation model zero-shot, or their own way.

KNOWN PITFALLS - check these first when a student reports a problem
- Frequency strings. pandas 3 removed the bare "M", "Q", "Y" and "H" aliases: use "MS" or "ME", "QS" or "QE", "YS" or "YE", and "h". "W" means week ending Sunday; use the frequency given in the series spec. After building a regular grid, check that the series did not come back empty.
- A series that ends with a long run of missing values: trim the tail to the last real observation instead of interpolating it.
- Seasonal naive for horizons longer than one season must repeat the last full season of the training data. Reading values from inside the holdout is leakage.
- Forecasting from the training part instead of the full history: the final forecast must be refitted on all the data, after the model was chosen on the validation or rolling windows.
- Log transforms: a model fitted on log values returns log forecasts. Exponentiate exactly once, both the forecast and the interval bounds.
- plot_acf fails once lags reach the length of the series, and does not handle NaN.
- STL needs seasonal to be an odd integer of at least 3.
- boxcox_lambda with method="loglik" rejects zero and negative values, and a multiplicative decomposition or ETS rejects them too. Zeros in a series are information about the data, not an error to hide.
- statsforecast requires every prediction level strictly between 0 and 100; ARIMA with a drift term needs exactly one difference in total.
- neuralforecast on a CPU runtime needs accelerator="cpu" together with devices=1. A small network on one short series changes a lot from seed to seed: compare two seeds before trusting one score.
- Foundation models: install the package versions pinned in the template; the first run downloads the model weights, which takes a few minutes in Colab.
- Submission files: check the column names, the number of rows, the dates and the interval bounds against the format section of the handout before uploading; the template's check cell is the same check the scorer uses.
