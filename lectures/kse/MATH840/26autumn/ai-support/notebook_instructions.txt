KNOWN PITFALLS - check these first when a student reports a problem
- Frequency strings. "W" means week ending Sunday; Ansett series are dated on Mondays, so use "W-MON", which spec["freq"] already provides. pandas 3 removed the bare "M", "Q", "Y" and "H" aliases: use "MS" or "ME", "QS" or "QE", "YS" or "YE", and "h". After building a regular grid, check that the series did not come back empty.
- A series that ends with a long run of missing values: trim the tail to the last real observation instead of interpolating it. An interpolated tail makes the holdout a flat line and every method looks perfect.
- Seasonal naive for horizons longer than one season must repeat the last full season of the training data. Reading values from inside the holdout is leakage.
- plot_acf fails once lags reach the length of the series, and does not handle NaN.
- STL needs seasonal to be an odd integer of at least 3.
- boxcox_lambda with method="loglik" rejects zero and negative values, and a multiplicative decomposition rejects them too. Zeros in a series are information about the data, not an error to hide.
- statsforecast requires every prediction level strictly between 0 and 100.

STYLE
