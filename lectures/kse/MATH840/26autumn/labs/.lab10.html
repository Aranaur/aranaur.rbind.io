TipWhat the model cards say they were trained on

You do not know your series’ source, but you know the families: Track B is cut from public Australian and US economic statistics. What each model publishes about its pretraining data:

Model Published training data
Chronos-2 Technical report, Table 6: 22 real datasets (M4 monthly, electricity and others) plus synthetic data

None of them lists Australian retail turnover or US employment statistics. “Not listed” is not “not seen”: a corpus can contain a series under another name. Say what you can and cannot rule out.

WarningWhere this goes wrong
  • Choosing on the validation window alone. Four windows, and the scoreboard’s mean — not window 1.
  • Reading one seed as the network’s score. A small network on one short series can move a lot from seed to seed. Run your best one twice.
  • “Foundation” read as “better”. On many Track B series ETS or ARIMA is still the one to beat.
  • Building the mix after reading the table. Then it is a selection in disguise. Write 2.3’s note first.
  • Judging an interval on the windows it was built from. interval_check leaves each window out.
  • Forecasting from train. The submission calls every forecaster again on the whole of y.
  • An API key in the notebook. TimeGPT is optional; if you use it, the key comes from a Colab secret.
