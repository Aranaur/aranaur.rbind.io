WarningWhere this goes wrong
  • Choosing the members after reading the table. Then the combination is a selection in disguise — the noisy decision it was meant to avoid. Write 2.2’s note first.
  • Comparing on the validation window alone. One window is one draw; the practice session’s tourism case showed selection on three windows picking the test winner barely more often than a coin.
  • Judging the conformal interval on the windows it was built from. It will look perfect. The template’s interval_check leaves each window out in turn — use it.
  • Averaging forecasts on different scales. Every member’s forecast must be on the original scale — fit_member takes care of the log; do not exponentiate twice.
  • Forecasting from train. The submission refits every member on the whole history.
